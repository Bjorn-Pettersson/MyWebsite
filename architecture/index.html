Background
I started my architecture career at the CITA institute for computational design, where I fell in love with computer science through a research assistant role working on a DQN implementation that eventually got published. I switched to strategic design in my second year for a more practical and project-driven approach, but continued algorithmic design work in my master's thesis 100M — a generative design collaboration with CARE Denmark. Realising I wanted to work more theoretically, I made the switch toward a more technical career with my degree at the University of Copenhagen.
